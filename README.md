# Film Production Society (FPS) — RV University

Welcome to the **Film Production Society** of RV University. We are a student-driven community of filmmakers, storytellers, and visual artists who believe that cinema is more than entertainment—it's a language, a craft, and a way to connect.

---

## 🎬 Who We Are

FPS is a creative hub for anyone passionate about filmmaking. Whether you're a beginner who's never held a camera or an experienced editor looking for collaborators, you'll find a place here. We bring together writers, directors, cinematographers, editors, sound designers, actors, and production designers from every discipline across the university.

Our philosophy is simple: **Create. Learn. Connect.**

---

## 🎥 What We Do

### 📽️ Original Productions
We develop and produce original short films, micro-films, and documentaries. From the first spark of an idea to the final export, FPS supports members through every stage of production.

### 🧠 Workshops & Skill-Building
Hands-on sessions covering:
- Directing & Blocking
- Cinematography & Lighting
- Sound Design & Recording
- Editing & Color Grading
- Screenwriting & Story Structure
- Acting for Camera

### 🎞️ Screenings & Discussions
We curate classic and contemporary cinema—from Satyajit Ray to Wong Kar-wai—and host post-screening discussions to analyze technique, theme, and storytelling.

### ⏱️ Filmmaking Challenges
24-hour, 48-hour, genre-based, silent, and smartphone challenges. These fast-paced events are designed to push creativity, build teamwork, and make films with minimal resources.

### 🎨 Art & Poster Design
Our visual team designs posters, title cards, and promotional materials that capture the soul of each film before a single frame is shot.
