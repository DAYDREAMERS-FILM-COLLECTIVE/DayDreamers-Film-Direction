Venue

The Screening Room

All club screenings are held on campus. The hall for each title is fixed and printed on the film card and on your reservation.

Hall 01

D Block, 3rd Floor

Main club screening room with rows A to G. Doors open thirty minutes before showtime. Members are requested to be seated ten minutes early.

Hall 02

D Block, 4th Floor

Second club screening room used for overflow and repeat shows. The hall name on your reservation always matches the room for that title.

House Rules

Watch Together

Phones away once the lights dim. No pause once the screening begins. Applause for the projection team is strongly encouraged.
