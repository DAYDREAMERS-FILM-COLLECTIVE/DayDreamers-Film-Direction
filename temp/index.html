The Manifesto

We believe cinema is direction — a gaze deliberately cast — and screening — a light deliberately shared. One cannot live without the other.

SCENE 01

Direction as Craft

Monthly master-sessions where members dissect framing, blocking and the choreography of the lens — from storyboard to final cut.

SCENE 02

Screening as Ritual

Every Friday at nine, the hall darkens. A single beam of light. No phones, no pause buttons — only the communal act of watching.

SCENE 03

The Club Room

Seventy velvet seats, a silver screen, and a wall of 35mm memories. Members direct, members project, members applaud.
