Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Daydreamers screenings, passes, and society activities.

Where can I find the privacy policy for Daydreamers screenings? ▾
Our privacy policy is available directly in the Legal Notices tab on this page. We collect only your student name, USN, and university email to generate cryptographic admission passes, with no marketing tracking.
Where can I find the Terms and Conditions for Daydreamers events? ▾
The full screening terms and conditions can be inspected under Legal Notices, detailing admission passes, auditorium decorum, and assigned seating rules.
Where can I find the imprint and club advisory information? ▾
The Imprint section details our student convenors, faculty mentors, and official campus location at D Block 4th Floor, RV University.
Can I attend Daydreamers screenings without registration or login? ▾
Yes! Daydreamers requires no user accounts, passwords, or registration. You simply select a movie, choose your seats, enter your student name and USN, and receive your digital ticket pass instantly.
Can I change my reserved seat after booking? ▾
During the reservation flow, you can click "Change Seats" to adjust selected seats on the seatmap. For already confirmed passes, visit the check-in desk at D Block 15 minutes before showtime.
Where are the screenings held? ▾
All screenings take place in the D Block 4th Floor Main Auditorium, RV University, fitted with custom acoustical damping and our 4K projection setup.
How can I submit my student film for screening? ▾
Student filmmakers from any department can submit short films, documentaries, or experimental reels by sending a screener link to hello@daydreamers.club or messaging via the Contact Us form.
I have another question or issue, how do I contact you? ▾
Switch to the Contact Us tab on this page to send a direct message, or email our society convenors at hello@daydreamers.club.
